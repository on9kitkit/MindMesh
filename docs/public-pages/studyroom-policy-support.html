Privacy, support, account deletion, and subscriptions

This repository-owned document is the canonical pre-publication source for StudyRoom's factual policy and support information. Public HTTPS publication is pending; this source does not claim a public URL.

Contact and support

The accountable owner is the StudyRoom project owner. For support, privacy, safety, or account-deletion questions, email studyroom.support@proton.me. The response target is within 3 working days. This is a support target, not a guaranteed emergency or crisis-response service.

For urgent real-world danger, contact local emergency services. In StudyRoom, a signed-in participant can report a safety concern about another participant from the current Waiting Room or Results flow. Reports are reviewed separately and do not automatically punish anyone.

What StudyRoom uses

StudyRoom uses a Supabase identity, email and authentication-session data, display name, room memberships, quiz participation, answers, results, timestamps, and necessary service metadata to authenticate members, run invite-only rooms, calculate results, and operate the service. RevenueCat and the relevant store handle purchase and entitlement records used for StudyRoom Pro.

Solo progress, confirmed coin and streak records, and pet ownership and equipment support the new self-paced study features. A learner's drawing workspace stays in app memory; strokes are not sent with an answer or stored by the backend.

StudyRoom application code does not currently collect or store location, contacts, photos, chat, direct messages, or public profiles. The current product does not use ads or usage-telemetry analytics. It can derive the signed-in learner's own bounded practice statistics on demand from retained quiz results, as described below. The app does not persist an entered password. Credentials, tokens, email addresses, and billing records are not returned by StudyRoom APIs or shown to room members.

Visibility and academic privacy

Rooms are invite-only. Current room members see the display names and room or quiz state needed for multiplayer. Quiz results remain scoped to the immutable participants of that quiz session; there is no public performance directory or cross-room profile.

Adaptive practice and your own summary

Adaptive GCSE quiz generation sends the selected subject, topic, mark budget, and a bounded list of recent question prompts to OpenAI to reduce repetition. Marking a written answer sends the question, any source extract, hidden rubric, and that answer. These requests carry no account, profile, room, or provider identifiers. StudyRoom requests that responses not be stored, but this is not a guarantee of zero provider retention. StudyRoom retains generated content, answers, and marks under its existing deletion and retention limits.

While signed in, a learner may view a private summary calculated on demand from their own still-retained finished adaptive GCSE sessions, including previous rooms they left or that closed. It labels accuracy on graded answers, shows unanswered and ungraded counts, and compares only sufficiently sampled topics within a subject. It does not create a separately stored analytics profile, disclose another learner's answers, or reopen a room's detailed answer review. Normal room-data purge or account deletion can remove source results; this is not a lifetime record, numeric AQA grade or full-syllabus measure.

Solo educational questions, answers, and marks become eligible for purge 30 days after completion, abandonment, or failure. Attempts still awaiting marking are not silently expired. Minimal earned reward, study-day, and pet ownership records remain until account deletion; a purge can run after its eligibility date rather than at the exact 30-day instant.

Account deletion

A signed-in member can start deletion in Account & settings, confirm the exact word DELETE, and complete recent authentication when requested. The authenticated StudyRoom deletion contract removes or anonymises local account data, preserves only the minimum tombstone and historical evidence needed for its authority, and schedules linked provider cleanup. This in-app route remains the primary deletion path.

If the app cannot be used, the monitored contact above is the external deletion-request entry. Include enough information for the owner to identify the request, but do not send passwords, access tokens, or payment credentials by email.

Subscriptions and purchases

RevenueCat and Apple or Google store systems control purchase, entitlement, receipt, and billing records. Deleting a StudyRoom account does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Manage or cancel a store subscription separately through the relevant store account. StudyRoom does not claim real-store availability or a subscription-management URL in this pre-publication source.

Retention and policy status

Current hosted retention and purge automation are not yet configured. The release policy must match the actual hosted and provider settings before publication. No retention setting, hosting region, provider guarantee, or legal-compliance conclusion is claimed here.

StudyRoom may update this source when its factual data use, deletion, support, or subscription boundaries change. Public HTTPS publication remains a separate release step.
