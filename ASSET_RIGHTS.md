## Adding final media

Record the actual creator/source, applicable licence or permission, date and permitted uses for each added asset. For an icon, retain the original artwork and any generation/source record. For a recording, check visible account details, notifications, room codes and credentials before publication. For third-party media, permission must cover the intended video and repository distribution, as applicable. Credit alone does not establish permission.

Keep signed contributor permissions and guardian documents in private records, outside this repository. Put only required, approved attribution and licence text into the public package. Do not assume the friend's helper/team status from their editing work.

The first-party policy, historical MIT grants and upstream notices serve different scopes. [GitHub's repository licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository) explains the role of a repository licence. Public visibility is not permission to relicense third-party work.
